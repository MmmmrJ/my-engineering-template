# Rights and compliance

Fail closed on uncertain rights. This workflow is production guidance, not legal advice.

## Allowed IP

Use only:

1. an original IP created and controlled by the user; or
2. an IP proven to be in the public domain for the intended jurisdiction and use.

For public-domain material, record the source URL or catalog record, author/death or publication facts, jurisdiction, public-domain rationale, and verification date. A familiar old story, an online copy, or a user assertion without evidence is not proof.

Do not proceed with copyrighted characters, franchises, scripts, illustrations, music, trademarks used as source identifiers, or close substitutes. Do not imitate a living artist's or director's signature style; translate direction into generic attributes such as palette, lens, lighting, composition, texture, and rhythm.

## People and voices

- Keep voice cloning disabled by default. A clone-capable path requires separate, specific consent evidence, an explicit user confirmation, and a persisted consent record.
- Do not synthesize a real person's identity, voice, or likeness.
- Use provider catalog voices or original recordings with documented commercial rights.
- Do not imply endorsement, authorship, or participation by a real person or brand.

## Asset provenance

For generated, stock, public-domain, or user-supplied media, preserve source, creator/provider, license or terms basis, model and request ID when applicable, creation/import date, prompt/settings lineage, and modifications. Review provider terms for training-input rights, commercial use, attribution, geography, retention, and prohibited content.

## Release checks

Before export, confirm:

- IP evidence remains attached to the task;
- every external asset and font has compatible rights;
- music, SFX, and voices permit the intended distribution;
- no watermark, unlicensed logo, private data, or accidental likeness remains;
- AI disclosure or labeling required by the platform or jurisdiction is prepared;
- provider terms and applicable law have been checked by the responsible human for commercial release.
